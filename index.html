ABOUT THE TOPIC

What is School Management?

🏫

Building Better Schools

School management refers to the administration and coordination of all activities within a school. It includes managing teachers, students, classrooms, resources and educational programmes.

Its main objective is to create a positive learning environment and improve the overall quality of education.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES

Functions of School Management

📋

Planning

Preparing academic calendars, timetables, school events and educational goals.

🏢

Organising

Arranging classrooms, resources, staff responsibilities and school activities.

👨‍🏫

Guiding

Providing proper guidance and motivation to teachers and students.

📊

Evaluating

Checking student performance and improving teaching and learning methods.
